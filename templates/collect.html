Collect training data

Record your own signs so the model learns from real examples. Each recording is 2 seconds. Only hand landmarks are saved on your computer (in data/samples.jsonl), never video.

Start the camera, choose what to record, then press Record. You get a 3-second countdown.

Tips for good data

  • At least 15 recordings per sign, varying angle and distance a little
  • Ask friends or family to record too (the trainer tests on people it hasn't seen)
  • Vary the lighting and background
  • Record a _nothing letter (hands resting) and a _neutral word so the model learns "no sign"
  • Check each sign against a trusted ASL source before recording it

Recordings so far

When you have enough, open a second terminal (with the venv active) and run python train.py. The app picks up the new model automatically.
