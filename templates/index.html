How it works

1

Show a sign

Turn on your camera. Nothing is recorded unless you choose to collect training data.

2

Hands become landmarks

MediaPipe finds 21 points on each hand, right in your browser. Only those numbers are sent to the server, never the video.

3

Landmarks become words

A small model predicts the sign and its confidence. Uncertain guesses are shown as uncertain, not as facts.

What this prototype does, and doesn't

Planned for the MVP

  • Live fingerspelling and a small word list (the vocabulary you train)
  • Confidence display, editable text, speech, history
  • Short uploaded clips, analysed over time

Not yet

  • Full conversations or sign-language grammar
  • Indian Sign Language (planned as a separate model)
  • Facial expressions and large vocabularies
  • Text-to-sign animations
